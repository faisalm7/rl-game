POLISCI 158 / Reinforcement learning

The feed

You have six posts to keep an audience informed without losing its trust. Can a reward for clicks ruin that?

The real goal: finish with information at 3 and trust at least 2. These are made-up rules for a small simulation, not claims about real audiences.

1. Play it

You start with trust 3 and information 0. Both stay between 0 and 3. Each button publishes one post. You can also use keys 1-4.

Choose a post to begin.

2. Choose the reward

Points are separate from the real goal. Changing any setting starts a fresh agent and game.

The default gives +2 for each information point actually gained, -3 per outrage post, +10 for the real goal at the end, and 0 per click. The second option changes only the click reward to +4.

3. Train the agent

112 table entries for states (7 round values x 4 trust levels x 4 information levels), with 4 actions each. This includes terminal states. It explores on 20% of training moves, learns at 0.2, and discounts later rewards by 0.95. Q-values start at zero and ties are broken randomly.
